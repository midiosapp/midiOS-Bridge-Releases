# midiOS Bridge 2.7.4 (1)

- Refreshed Layout Manager previews to match the current midiOS instrument in portrait and landscape.
- Shows the saved bank colors and selected bank, with updated artwork for controls including XY pads, toggles, envelopes, and Jump pads.
- Distinguishes CC, Program Change, and SysEx pads and shows saved layout targets on Jump pads.

Requires macOS 15.6 or later.
